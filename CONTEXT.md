# Calendar Invite Campaign Platform

A SaaS product for webinar organisers who need to send calendar invitations at scale to opted-in audiences and measure whether registrants actually attend.

## Language

**Organisation**:
A customer-owned workspace that contains users, mailboxes, contacts, campaigns, billing, and audit records.
_Avoid_: Account, tenant

**User**:
A person who signs in to the platform and acts inside an organisation.
_Avoid_: Mailbox, sender

**Mailbox**:
A Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 email account connected by a customer and used to send calendar invitations.
_Avoid_: User account, sending account

**Audience**:
A set of contacts imported for a campaign.
_Avoid_: List, spreadsheet

**Contact**:
A person in an audience who may receive a calendar invitation if suppression and deliverability rules allow it.
_Avoid_: Recipient, lead

**Suppression List**:
The permanent block list of contacts who must not receive invitations, usually because they unsubscribed.
_Avoid_: Unsubscribe list, blacklist

**Campaign**:
The customer's configured webinar invitation effort, including event details, audience, send plan, and tracking.
_Avoid_: Event, blast

**Send Plan**:
The approved day-by-day assignment of contacts to mailboxes based on capacity, provider matching, and scheduling rules.
_Avoid_: Schedule, queue

**Invitation Attempt**:
A durable record of an attempt to create, update, or withdraw a calendar invitation for one contact.
_Avoid_: Send job, delivery

**Native Calendar Mode**:
A sending mode where the platform creates calendar invitations directly through Google Calendar API or Microsoft Graph Calendar using connected customer mailboxes.
_Avoid_: API mode, direct mode

**Calendar Email Mode**:
A high-volume sending mode where the platform sends calendar invitation emails through an email provider using calendar invite payloads.
_Avoid_: SendGrid mode, ICS mode

**Hosted RSVP**:
A platform-owned response flow where a contact records accept, decline, or tentative intent through signed links controlled by the platform.
_Avoid_: RSVP link, tracking page

**Deliverability Gate**:
The pre-launch readiness check and ongoing health state that decides whether a calendar email campaign may send, must slow down, pause, or be blocked based on sender authentication, unsubscribe, webhook, suppression, warm-up, and reputation signals.
_Avoid_: Spam check, email checklist

**Campaign Health**:
The versioned, evidence-backed assessment of a campaign's current sending safety, including authentication, provider webhook freshness, delivery, bounce, deferral, complaint, unsubscribe, and reputation signals.
_Avoid_: Deliverability score

**Delivery Bucket**:
The recipient traffic group used for pacing, derived from the recipient's mailbox provider/MX host as well as its individual domain. It prevents separately named Google- or Microsoft-hosted domains from being incorrectly treated as independent capacity pools.
_Avoid_: Recipient domain limit

**Branded Tracking Domain**:
A customer-owned HTTPS domain used for RSVP and tracking links so recipients and mailbox providers see links aligned with the sender's brand.
_Avoid_: Tracking URL, redirect domain

**Sending Domain**:
A customer-owned domain or subdomain verified for calendar email sending, return-path handling, branded tracking, and hosted RSVP.
_Avoid_: Sender, email domain

**Calendar Email Template**:
The customer-visible email and calendar invite content generated by the platform for calendar email mode, including HTML, plain text, and ICS payloads.
_Avoid_: SendGrid template, email design

**Audience Import**:
The process of turning a customer-provided CSV or XLSX file into a validated audience with mapped fields, deduplicated contacts, provider detection, and rejected rows.
_Avoid_: Upload, spreadsheet import

**Meeting Provider**:
The webinar or meeting system used for a campaign, such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, or a custom meeting URL.
_Avoid_: Webinar tool, meeting type

**Attendance Sync**:
The process of retrieving post-event attendance from the campaign's meeting provider and matching it back to invited contacts.
_Avoid_: Report import, attendee tracking
