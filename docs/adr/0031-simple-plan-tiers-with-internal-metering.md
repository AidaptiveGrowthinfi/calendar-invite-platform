# Use simple plan tiers with internal usage metering

The MVP will use simple subscription plan tiers rather than usage-based billing or automatic overage charges. The exact plan names, prices, and limits remain a product-owner decision, but the application will meter usage internally for invites, verified contacts, attendance syncs, connected mailboxes, sending domains, team seats, and shared-domain trial sends so limits can be enforced and future usage-based pricing remains possible.
